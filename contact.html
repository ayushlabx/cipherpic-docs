Questions, feedback, or a security report — here's how to reach us and what to include so we can help as quickly as possible.

General Support

Privacy Requests

To exercise a privacy right described in our Privacy Policy — such as accessing, correcting, or deleting your information — email us with enough detail to help us understand and process your request. We may ask for additional information to verify your identity before responding.

Email: cipherpicapp@gmail.com

Security Vulnerability Disclosure

If you've found a security issue in CipherPic, we welcome a responsible report rather than public disclosure. Please include:

  • A description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce it
  • Your device model and operating system version
  • The CipherPic version you're using
  • Screenshots or recordings, if helpful

Email: cipherpicapp@gmail.com
