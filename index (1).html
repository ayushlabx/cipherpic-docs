A private vault that stays on your device.

CipherPic protects your photos, documents, notes, passwords, and cards behind encryption and strong authentication — with a local-first design that keeps your information under your control, not ours.

Get Started Read our Privacy Policy

Built around a simple idea

Most apps collect more than they need. CipherPic is designed the other way around — your vault contents are encrypted and stored primarily on your own device, and we generally have no way to read, recover, or view them.

What CipherPic protects

Photos & documents

Private photographs, identity documents, and personal files, encrypted before they're stored.

Passwords & accounts

Account credentials, recovery codes, and two-factor backup information in one protected place.

Cards & notes

Payment cards, membership cards, and secure notes — organized with tags and pinned items.

Peace of mind

PIN and biometric unlock, screenshot protection, decoy vaults, and an emergency wipe when you need it.
