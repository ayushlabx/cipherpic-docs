Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 7, 2026

Welcome to CipherPic. Your privacy is at the heart of everything we build. CipherPic is designed to help you securely store, organize, protect, and share sensitive images and personal information while giving you complete control over your data.

Unlike traditional cloud photo storage applications, CipherPic follows a Privacy-First approach. Most of your encrypted information remains stored securely on your own device. When online features are used, they are designed to minimize the information we collect and maximize your control over your own data.

2. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CipherPic collects, stores, protects, processes, and uses your information.

This policy applies to:

  • CipherPic Android application
  • CipherPic online authentication services
  • CipherPic secure sharing features
  • CipherPic official website
  • Future official CipherPic releases

It does not apply to third-party applications or websites that are not operated by CipherPic.

3. Our Privacy Philosophy

CipherPic was built around one simple idea:

Your Data Belongs To You.

We believe your private memories, identification documents, passwords, financial information, and sensitive images should remain under your control—not ours.

Whenever technically possible:

  • Your encrypted files stay on your own device.
  • Your encryption keys stay under your control.
  • Your vault passwords remain protected.
  • Your private images are never intentionally viewed by us.
  • Your sensitive information is never sold.

4. Definitions

Throughout this Privacy Policy:

  • Application means the CipherPic mobile application.
  • Vault means an encrypted storage area inside CipherPic.
  • User means anyone using CipherPic.
  • Account means your online CipherPic account.
  • Encrypted Data means information protected using encryption.
  • Local Storage means information stored only on your own device.
  • Online Services means optional internet-based features.
  • Personal Information means information that can identify you, such as your email address.

5. Summary

Before reading the complete Privacy Policy, here is a quick summary:

  • ✅ Most of your encrypted data stays on your own device.
  • ✅ We do not intentionally view your private images.
  • ✅ We do not sell your personal information.
  • ✅ We use Firebase Authentication only for secure login.
  • ✅ Online sharing is designed to securely transfer encrypted files.
  • ✅ You remain in control of your encrypted vaults.

6. Information We Collect

CipherPic follows a data minimization approach. We only collect information that is necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

The information collected depends on whether you use CipherPic in Offline Mode or Online Mode.

Offline Users

If you use CipherPic entirely offline, almost all of your sensitive information remains only on your device.

We do not automatically receive copies of your encrypted images, vaults, passwords, notes, documents, cards, or other private information.

Online Users

If you create a CipherPic account and use online services, certain information is required to provide those features securely.

This may include:

  • Email address
  • Encrypted account identifier
  • Authentication tokens
  • Device identifier used for security purposes
  • Share metadata required to deliver encrypted files

We collect only the minimum amount of information required to operate these services.

7. Information We Do NOT Collect

CipherPic is intentionally designed so that we do not intentionally collect or access your sensitive personal content.

Unless you explicitly choose to use an online feature that requires processing, we do not intentionally collect:

  • Your decrypted images
  • Your vault PIN
  • Your vault passwords
  • Your encryption keys
  • Your biometric information
  • Your Secure Notes
  • Your Cards Vault contents
  • Your Clipboard Vault contents
  • Your Hidden Vault contents
  • Your Decoy Vault contents
  • Your recovery answers
  • Your local activity history
  • Your local access logs

8. Firebase Authentication

CipherPic uses Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, to securely create and manage user accounts.

Firebase Authentication may process:

  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials
  • Authentication tokens
  • Unique account identifier

CipherPic never receives or stores your Google account password.

Authentication is securely handled by Firebase according to Google's security standards.

9. Local Storage

Most CipherPic information is stored locally on your own device.

Examples include:

  • Encrypted Images
  • Vault Information
  • Secure Notes
  • Password Vault
  • Accounts Vault
  • Cards Vault
  • Clipboard Vault
  • Pinned Items
  • Tags
  • Access Logs
  • Activity History
  • Privacy Health status
  • Recovery information
  • Security settings

This information normally never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to use a feature that requires secure online communication.

10. Encryption

CipherPic encrypts sensitive information before storing it.

Encryption helps protect your information from unauthorized access if your device is lost, stolen, or compromised.

Encryption keys are designed to remain under your control whenever technically possible.

CipherPic is designed so that the developer cannot intentionally read your encrypted vault contents.

11. Passwords & Recovery

Vault PINs and passwords are never intentionally stored in plain text.

Recovery information is protected using secure storage mechanisms appropriate for its purpose.

Users are responsible for selecting strong passwords and keeping recovery information confidential.

12. Offline Mode

CipherPic can operate almost entirely without an internet connection.

When operating offline:

  • No images are uploaded.
  • No vault contents are uploaded.
  • No passwords are uploaded.
  • No notes are uploaded.
  • No card information is uploaded.
  • No clipboard information is uploaded.
  • No private documents are uploaded.

Your encrypted information remains stored only on your own device unless you explicitly decide to use online features.

13. Permissions We Request

CipherPic requests only the permissions required to provide its features. Permissions are used solely for the functionality you choose to use.

Permission Purpose
Camera Capture photos securely, scan documents, and support Login Audit Camera.
Photos & Media Import and export images selected by the user.
Storage Store encrypted vault files and import user-selected files.
Biometric Authentication Unlock vaults using fingerprint or face authentication.
Notifications Security alerts, reminders, and optional share notifications.

14. Camera Usage

Camera access is only used when you choose to capture a photo or scan a document inside CipherPic.

If Login Audit Camera is enabled, the camera may capture a photo after repeated failed unlock attempts to help identify unauthorized access.

Camera images are never captured without the conditions required for that feature.

15. Photos & Media Access

CipherPic requests access to your photos and media only when you choose to import or export files.

The application does not automatically upload your gallery contents or continuously scan your photo library.

16. Biometric Authentication

CipherPic can use your device's built-in biometric authentication (such as fingerprint or facial recognition) to unlock protected content.

Your biometric data is never collected, stored, or transmitted by CipherPic.

Biometric verification is handled entirely by your device's secure operating system.

17. Notifications

Notifications are optional and may be used for:

  • Security alerts
  • Share notifications
  • Document expiry reminders
  • Backup reminders
  • Privacy Health recommendations

You may disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

18. Secure Sharing

CipherPic supports secure sharing of encrypted files between users.

Shared files are intended to remain encrypted during transmission whenever supported by the service being used.

Recipients must satisfy the required authentication or password requirements before access is granted.

Depending on the selected sharing options, shared items may include:

  • Expiration dates
  • View limits
  • View-once protection
  • Custom passwords
  • Watermark policies
  • Access logging

19. Watermarks

CipherPic allows users to apply visible and invisible watermarks to shared content.

Watermarks are generated according to the options selected by the user and may include information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Recipient
  • Timestamp
  • Session ID
  • Custom text

Watermark information is controlled by the user and is only applied when selected.

20. Device Security Features

CipherPic includes numerous privacy and security features designed to help protect sensitive information.

  • Encrypted vaults
  • Hidden Vault
  • Decoy Vault
  • Duress PIN
  • Duress Images
  • Camouflage Mode
  • Screenshot Protection
  • Rotate Encryption Keys
  • Privacy Health
  • Emergency Wipe
  • Access Logs
  • Login Audit Camera

Most of these features operate entirely on your device and are designed to improve the protection of your private information.

21. Data Retention

CipherPic retains information only for as long as necessary to provide the services you choose to use.

Local encrypted data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the application.

Authentication records associated with your online account may remain until your account is permanently deleted.

22. Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your CipherPic account at any time from within the application.

Deleting your account will remove your authentication account and associated online account information.

Deleting your online account does not automatically erase encrypted local vaults stored on your device unless you also choose to remove them.

23. Emergency Wipe

CipherPic includes an Emergency Wipe feature that allows users to permanently erase encrypted vault data stored on their device.

Emergency Wipe is irreversible. Once completed, deleted encrypted information cannot be recovered.

24. Third-Party Services

CipherPic may use trusted third-party services only where necessary to provide requested functionality.

Examples include:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (optional notifications, if enabled)

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

26. International Users
