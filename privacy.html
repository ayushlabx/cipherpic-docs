1. Introduction

CipherPic is a privacy-focused application designed to help individuals securely store, organize, and protect sensitive personal information using modern encryption and strong authentication.

CipherPic allows users to securely manage private photographs, identity documents, secure notes, passwords, account credentials, payment and membership cards, clipboard entries, and other confidential information inside encrypted vaults.

The application has been developed with a privacy-first and local-first architecture. Rather than relying on continuous cloud storage or unnecessary collection of personal information, CipherPic is designed to process and protect your information primarily on your own device.

Security features available within CipherPic include encrypted vault storage, biometric authentication (where supported), PIN protection, automatic vault locking, screenshot protection, secure trash, emergency wipe, activity history, privacy health checks, secure import and export tools, multiple independent vaults, decoy protection features, login audit camera, and additional security controls intended to help users maintain control over their private information.

Our goal is simple: to provide a secure environment where users — not advertisers, analytics companies, or data brokers — remain in control of their own sensitive information. CipherPic is continuously improved to strengthen security, improve usability, address newly discovered vulnerabilities, and adapt to evolving privacy standards while maintaining our commitment to protecting user data.

2. Definitions

To help you understand this Privacy Policy, the following terms are used throughout:

  • "CipherPic," "we," "us," or "our" refers to the developer and operator of the CipherPic application.
  • "You" or "user" refers to any individual who downloads, installs, or uses CipherPic.
  • "Personal information" means information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with an identifiable individual.
  • "Vault" means an encrypted storage area within CipherPic where you may store photographs, documents, notes, passwords, cards, and other protected content.
  • "Protected content" or "vault contents" means the information you choose to store inside a vault.
  • "Local-first" means that information is processed and stored primarily on your own device rather than on remote servers.
  • "Encryption" means the process of converting information into a coded form that cannot be read without the appropriate decryption key or successful authentication.
  • "Export" or "backup" means a file you intentionally create through CipherPic to store or move your information outside the application.
  • "Device" means the smartphone, tablet, or other hardware on which CipherPic is installed.

3. Our Privacy Philosophy

At CipherPic, we believe that privacy is a fundamental right, not a premium feature.

Many applications collect extensive information about their users to support advertising, behavioral profiling, or commercial analytics. CipherPic was designed with a different philosophy. Our goal is to give you a secure place to protect your most sensitive information while collecting as little information as reasonably possible.

Privacy by Design

Privacy considerations are integrated into the design, development, and operation of CipherPic from the beginning. Security and privacy are treated as core requirements rather than optional additions.

Local-First Processing

Whenever possible, CipherPic processes and stores your protected information locally on your device. Your encrypted vault content is intended to remain under your control rather than being continuously transmitted to external servers.

Data Minimization

CipherPic is designed to collect only the information necessary to provide its features. We do not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information, excessive device information, or unrelated usage data.

User Control

You remain in control of your information. CipherPic provides features that allow you to manage, export, back up, restore, or permanently delete your protected data according to your own preferences.

Strong Security

CipherPic uses modern security technologies and multiple layers of protection to help safeguard your information against unauthorized access. These protections include encryption, authentication mechanisms, secure storage, and additional privacy controls designed to reduce the risk of accidental or unauthorized disclosure.

Transparency

We believe users should understand how their information is handled. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices in clear language and is intended to provide transparency regarding the operation of CipherPic.

Continuous Improvement

Privacy and security are continuously evolving. As CipherPic grows, we may improve our security architecture, encryption methods, authentication systems, and privacy protections to address new technologies, changing regulations, and emerging security threats. Such improvements are intended to strengthen user protection while respecting the principles described in this Privacy Policy.

4. Information We Collect

CipherPic has been designed to minimize the collection of personal information. Most information processed by the application exists solely to provide its security, storage, and organizational features. Depending on how you use CipherPic, the application may process the following categories of information.

Information You Choose to Store

CipherPic allows you to voluntarily store sensitive information inside encrypted vaults, such as private photographs and images, identity documents, secure notes, passwords and recovery codes, website accounts, payment and membership cards, clipboard entries you save, and tags or other organizational labels. CipherPic does not automatically create this information — it exists only because you intentionally choose to import, create, or save it.

Authentication Information

To protect your encrypted vaults, CipherPic may process authentication-related information, including your application PIN, biometric authentication status (when supported by your device), auto-lock configuration, and recovery configuration. Authentication credentials are used only for securing access to your vaults and related security features.

Application Settings

CipherPic stores configuration preferences you select, such as auto-lock duration, notification preferences, screenshot protection settings, backup preferences, and vault organization preferences.

Security Logs

To help you monitor access to your protected information, CipherPic may maintain encrypted security records, including Activity History, login and authentication events, and Login Audit Camera records (when enabled). These records are intended to help users review important security-related activity within the application.

Imported Content

When you use CipherPic's Import Wizard, the application may process information selected by you from sources such as your camera, device gallery, device storage, or clipboard. CipherPic only imports content that you intentionally select.

Backup and Export Files

If you choose to create encrypted backups or export your information, CipherPic may generate encrypted backup files containing the data you selected. These files remain under your control.

Device Information

CipherPic may access limited device information necessary for secure operation, such as device model, operating system version, application version, and security capability information (such as biometric support). CipherPic does not intentionally collect unnecessary device identifiers for advertising, profiling, or commercial analytics.

Information You Provide When Contacting Us

If you voluntarily contact CipherPic Support, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your email address, the content of your support request, and any screenshots you voluntarily attach. We use this information solely to respond to your request and improve support services.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

CipherPic has been designed to minimize data collection and avoid processing information that is not required for its intended functionality. Unless explicitly described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy, CipherPic does not intentionally collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information for advertising, profiling, or unrelated commercial purposes. In particular, CipherPic is designed not to intentionally:

  • Sell your personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies.
  • Build advertising, behavioral, or marketing profiles based on your private information.
  • Read your private photos, documents, notes, passwords, or cards outside of actions you intentionally perform within the app.
  • Upload the contents of your encrypted vaults for advertising purposes.
  • Analyze your encrypted content to determine which advertisements to show you.
  • Share sensitive vault data with unrelated third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.
  • Collect unnecessary personal information beyond what a feature you use requires.
  • Access device features without permission.
  • Monitor your activity outside of CipherPic, including browsing history, messages, calls, or other apps.
  • Access your protected information without successful authentication.
  • Retain deleted information longer than necessary.

While we strive to minimize data collection wherever possible, certain technical information may still be processed as necessary to provide application functionality, maintain security, comply with applicable law, investigate abuse, or respond to valid legal obligations. Any such processing is described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy.

8. Local Storage & Encryption

Local Storage

Unless a feature explicitly requires otherwise, the information you store in CipherPic — including photographs, secure notes, passwords, account credentials, payment cards, personal documents, clipboard entries, and tags — is stored locally on your device. CipherPic is designed to keep your protected information under your control rather than continuously synchronizing it with external servers. CipherPic is not designed to continuously upload your protected vault contents to cloud storage as part of its normal operation.

Encryption

CipherPic uses modern cryptographic techniques to help protect your sensitive information while it is stored by the application. Depending on the type of protected data and the feature being used, CipherPic employs multiple layers of security, including strong authenticated encryption for protected vault content, secure cryptographic key generation, device-backed key protection where supported by the operating system, and secure PIN-based authentication. These measures are intended to ensure that protected information cannot be easily accessed without successful authentication.

Temporary Processing

Certain operations — such as importing files, viewing encrypted images, or restoring backups — may require CipherPic to temporarily process information in device memory. CipherPic is designed to minimize the amount of time sensitive information remains available during these operations and to clear temporary working data whenever practical once the operation completes.

Secure Deletion

CipherPic provides features intended to help users permanently remove protected information, including Secure Trash and Emergency Wipe. When content is permanently deleted, CipherPic is designed to remove it from its managed storage, though complete removal may also depend on factors outside the application's control, such as the operating system, storage hardware, or backups you have created.

10. Data Retention

CipherPic retains information only for as long as necessary to provide the functionality you choose to use, maintain application security, comply with applicable legal obligations, or protect the rights of CipherPic and its users. Because CipherPic is designed as a local-first application, most protected information remains stored on your own device until you decide to modify or remove it.

Information Stored in Your Vault

Encrypted content remains stored on your device until you delete it, permanently erase it from Secure Trash, perform an Emergency Wipe, or remove the application and its data from your device. CipherPic does not automatically remove your protected information unless a feature specifically requires it or you instruct it to do so.

Secure Trash

Items moved to Secure Trash remain available until you permanently delete them, empty Secure Trash, or a configured automatic cleanup period expires (if enabled).

Activity History

Activity History records are retained only as long as necessary to provide the feature. You may delete your Activity History at any time through application settings, where supported.

Login Audit Records

If Login Audit Camera or related features are enabled, captured security records remain stored until you delete them.

Support Communications

If you contact CipherPic Support, we may retain your communications for a reasonable period necessary to respond to your request, resolve technical issues, and comply with legal obligations.

Legal Retention

In limited circumstances, we may retain certain information for longer periods where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, dispute resolution, or the protection of legal rights.

11. Exports & Backups

CipherPic allows users to create encrypted backups and export selected information when they choose to do so. Backups and exported files are created only upon user request. You are responsible for deciding where these files are stored, how they are protected, and with whom they are shared.

Backups

Encrypted backups created by CipherPic remain available until you delete them. CipherPic cannot automatically delete backup files saved outside the application, such as those stored in cloud storage or external devices. You are responsible for managing and securely deleting exported backup files.

Exported Data

Information exported from CipherPic remains outside the application's control once exported. You are responsible for storing, protecting, sharing, and deleting exported files.

Right to Export Your Information

CipherPic provides an Export My Data feature that allows you to export supported information under your control. Exported files remain your responsibility once they leave the application.

We recommend storing exported backups securely and deleting backups you no longer need.

12. Your Rights

CipherPic is designed to give you meaningful control over your information. Depending on your location and the laws that apply to you, you may have certain privacy rights regarding your personal information. Because CipherPic is primarily a local-first application, many privacy controls are available directly within the application itself.

Right to Access

You may request access to the personal information we process about you, where applicable. In many cases, information stored locally within CipherPic can already be viewed directly through the application.

Right to Correct Information

You may update, modify, or correct the information you have stored within CipherPic at any time, including secure notes, passwords, account credentials, cards, tags, and security settings.

Right to Delete Information

You may delete your information at any time using the tools provided by CipherPic, including deleting individual vault items, emptying Secure Trash, removing Activity History, deleting backups, or performing an Emergency Wipe. Exported files or backups stored outside CipherPic must be deleted separately by you.

Right to Restrict Processing

Where permitted by applicable law, you may request that we restrict certain processing of your personal information. Because CipherPic performs most processing locally, many forms of processing can also be limited by disabling optional features, revoking permissions, or uninstalling the application.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Where CipherPic relies on your consent for optional features or permissions — such as camera access, notifications, or biometric authentication — you may withdraw that consent at any time through your device settings or within CipherPic. Withdrawing certain permissions may prevent related features from functioning correctly.

Right to Object

Where permitted by applicable law, you may object to certain processing of your personal information. We will consider such requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Right to Data Portability

Where applicable, you may request a copy of your personal information in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. CipherPic's export functionality is intended to assist users in managing and transferring their own information.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, we encourage you to contact us first so we have an opportunity to investigate and resolve your concerns. Depending on your country or region, you may also have the right to submit a complaint to your local data protection authority.

Exercising Your Rights

If you wish to exercise any applicable privacy rights, you may contact us using the information provided at the end of this Privacy Policy. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

16. Security Measures

Protecting your information is the primary purpose of CipherPic. We have designed the application with multiple layers of privacy and security intended to reduce unauthorized access to your protected information. While no software or security system can guarantee absolute security, CipherPic is built using a security-first approach.

Authentication Protection

CipherPic supports multiple authentication mechanisms designed to help prevent unauthorized access, including application PIN protection, biometric authentication (where supported), automatic application locking, and authentication before revealing sensitive passwords or changing critical security settings.

Device Security Integration

CipherPic uses security capabilities provided by your operating system where available, including the Android Keystore, hardware-backed cryptographic protection, secure credential storage, and biometric security frameworks. Biometric information is processed by your device's operating system and is never collected or stored by CipherPic.

Cryptographic Key Protection

CipherPic is designed to protect encryption keys separately from encrypted user content wherever reasonably possible. Access to encrypted information depends on successful user authentication and the secure handling of cryptographic keys.

Software Integrity

We regularly improve CipherPic to address software defects, improve security, strengthen privacy protections, and enhance overall reliability. Users are encouraged to install updates from official distribution channels to receive the latest security improvements.

No Absolute Security Guarantee

Despite our efforts, no application, device, network, or encryption system can guarantee complete protection against every possible security threat. Accordingly, while CipherPic is designed with multiple layers of security and privacy protection, we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of information will never occur. We continually work to improve the security of CipherPic and encourage users to follow good security practices.

17. User Responsibilities & Limitation of Liability

CipherPic has been designed with privacy, security, and local data protection as its primary objectives. While we strive to provide a secure and reliable application, users are ultimately responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices and the information they choose to store.

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Unless you intentionally export, share, back up, or otherwise transmit your information, your encrypted vault contents remain stored locally on your own device. We do not routinely upload, copy, monitor, or maintain your private vault contents on our servers, and we cannot read, recover, or view this information during normal operation of the application.

Your Responsibility

You are responsible for protecting your device, your device passcode, your CipherPic PIN, your recovery information, and any exported or backup files you create. If another person gains physical or remote access to your unlocked device, the security of your information may be affected.

Malware and Device Security

CipherPic cannot protect your information if your device has been compromised by malware, spyware, unauthorized system modifications, rooting, or jailbreaking. We strongly recommend installing applications only from trusted sources, keeping your operating system updated, and avoiding unknown or modified applications.

Device Loss or Damage

CipherPic cannot recover encrypted information if your device is permanently damaged or factory reset, your encrypted backups are lost, your recovery information is forgotten, or encryption keys become permanently unavailable. Users are responsible for creating and securely storing backups if they wish to retain access to their information.

No Access to Your Private Content

Because CipherPic is designed around user-controlled encryption and local storage, we generally cannot access your encrypted vault, recover forgotten PINs, view your photographs, restore deleted local data, or unlock your vault for you. This is intentional and helps ensure your personal information remains under your control.

Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, CipherPic and its developer shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages arising from or related to loss of personal data, forgotten PINs or recovery information, device or hardware failure, software corruption, malware or unauthorized third-party software, theft or loss of a device, unauthorized access caused by user negligence, or events beyond our reasonable control. Nothing in this Privacy Policy limits any rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable consumer protection or privacy laws.

Use at Your Own Responsibility

By choosing to use CipherPic, you acknowledge that you understand the application's local-first design and agree to use it responsibly. Maintaining a secure device, practicing good cybersecurity habits, and keeping reliable backups remain essential responsibilities of every user.
