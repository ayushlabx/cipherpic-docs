1. Introduction

1. Introduction

Welcome to CipherPic ("CipherPic", "we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is the foundation of everything we build. Unlike many applications, CipherPic is designed with a privacy-first and local-first architecture, meaning your sensitive information is intended to remain on your own device under your control.

This Privacy Policy explains how CipherPic collects, uses, stores, protects, and processes information when you use our application. It also explains your rights, our responsibilities, and the security measures implemented to help safeguard your information.

CipherPic is designed for users who wish to securely store private photographs, documents, secure notes, passwords, account credentials, payment cards, clipboard entries, and other sensitive information in an encrypted vault.

Unlike many cloud-based services, CipherPic is designed so that your encrypted vault contents remain stored locally on your device unless you intentionally export, share, or back up your information.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using CipherPic. By installing or using the application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the following terms have the meanings described below unless the context requires otherwise.

2.1 CipherPic

"CipherPic", "the App", "we", "our", or "us" refers to the CipherPic mobile application and its associated documentation, website, and services provided by the developer.

2.2 User

"You", "your", or "user" refers to any individual who installs, accesses, or uses CipherPic.

2.3 Device

A Device refers to the smartphone, tablet, or other compatible hardware on which CipherPic is installed and used.

2.4 Vault

A Vault is the encrypted storage area within CipherPic where users securely store sensitive information, including photographs, documents, notes, passwords, account credentials, payment cards, clipboard entries, and other supported content.

2.5 Personal Data

Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, including information that users choose to store within CipherPic.

2.6 Encrypted Content

Encrypted Content refers to all information secured using CipherPic's encryption systems before being stored within the application.

2.7 Local Storage

Local Storage means information stored directly on your own device rather than on servers controlled by CipherPic.

2.8 Backup

A Backup refers to an encrypted copy of your vault that you intentionally create for recovery or transfer purposes.

2.9 Export

An Export is a user-initiated process that creates a copy of selected information outside the encrypted vault for sharing, migration, or personal storage.

2.10 Encryption Keys

Encryption Keys are cryptographic values used to encrypt and decrypt protected information stored within CipherPic. These keys are designed to protect your data from unauthorized access.

3. Our Privacy Philosophy

At CipherPic, privacy is not an optional feature. It is the foundation of how the application is designed.

CipherPic follows a privacy-first and local-first approach. The application is designed to keep your sensitive information under your control by storing protected content directly on your device instead of relying on unnecessary cloud storage.

Your private content, including images, passwords, notes, cards, accounts, and other vault items, is designed to remain encrypted and protected within your device's secure storage environment.

CipherPic does not have access to the contents of your personal vault. We cannot view, read, or recover your stored images, passwords, notes, documents, or other encrypted information because your private data is protected by encryption mechanisms controlled by your device and your security credentials.

CipherPic is designed with the principle that your private information belongs to you. The application does not intentionally collect, sell, or use your personal vault contents for advertising, profiling, tracking, or other commercial purposes.

Security and privacy features are provided to help reduce unauthorized access risks. However, users remain responsible for protecting their device, authentication methods, recovery information, and exported backups.

While CipherPic implements security measures designed to protect your information, no digital system can guarantee absolute protection against every possible threat, including device compromise, malware, unauthorized access, physical loss, or user mistakes.

We encourage users to maintain good security practices, including keeping their operating system updated, avoiding malicious applications, protecting their PINs and recovery information, and only sharing encrypted backups with trusted parties.

4. Information We Collect

CipherPic is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. Most information processed by the application exists only to provide security, storage, and organizational features requested by the user.

Depending on how you use CipherPic, the application may process the following categories of information:

Information You Choose to Store

CipherPic allows you to voluntarily store sensitive information inside encrypted vaults. This may include:

  • Private photographs and images
  • Identity documents
  • Passports and visas
  • Personal documents
  • Secure notes
  • Passwords and account credentials
  • Recovery codes and security information
  • Payment cards and membership cards
  • Clipboard entries saved by you
  • Tags, labels, and organizational information
  • Other information intentionally imported or created by you

CipherPic does not automatically create this information. It exists only because you choose to import, create, or save it within the application.

Authentication Information

To protect your encrypted vault, CipherPic may process security-related information such as:

  • Application PIN
  • Biometric authentication status (when supported by your device)
  • Auto-lock settings
  • Security preferences
  • Recovery configuration (if enabled)

Authentication information is used only for protecting access to your vault and enabling security features.
