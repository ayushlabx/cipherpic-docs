Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026


Our Commitment

CipherPic was built with one goal: Your privacy comes first. Your personal images and sensitive information belong only to you.


Offline Storage

In Offline Mode, all encrypted vaults, images, passwords, notes, cards, and documents remain on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.


Encryption

CipherPic encrypts your sensitive data before storage. We cannot view, recover, or access your encrypted vault contents.


Firebase Authentication

If you create an online account, Firebase Authentication securely stores your login credentials. Your vault contents remain separate from authentication data.


Online Sharing

When using secure sharing, encrypted files are transferred securely. Only the intended recipient can decrypt shared content.


Permissions

  • Camera (secure capture)
  • Photos & Storage
  • Biometric Authentication
  • Notifications (optional)

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Your images
  • Your passwords
  • Your PINs
  • Your encryption keys
  • Your vault contents
