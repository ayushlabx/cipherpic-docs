Delete Your CipherPic Account

You can permanently delete your CipherPic account at any time.


How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open CipherPic.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account.
  4. Verify your identity.
  5. Confirm permanent deletion.

What Happens?

  • Your CipherPic account will be permanently removed.
  • Your authentication account will be deleted.
  • Pending online shares may become inaccessible.
  • Local encrypted vaults remain on your device until you choose to erase them.
