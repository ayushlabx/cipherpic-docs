Getting Started

The basics of setting up and using CipherPic.

📱 Can I use CipherPic without the internet?

Yes. Almost all vault features — creating vaults, storing photos and notes, PIN and biometric unlock, and organizing content — work completely offline. An internet connection is only needed for optional features such as app updates or, if enabled, server-relayed sharing.

📋 How do I import files?

Tap the + button within a vault and follow the Import Security Wizard. You can import from your camera, photo gallery, device storage, or clipboard. Only the content you select is imported — CipherPic does not scan your device automatically.

🔐 How do I set up my vault PIN?

You'll be prompted to create a PIN the first time you open CipherPic. We recommend choosing a PIN that isn't used anywhere else, and enabling biometric unlock (if your device supports it) for faster access without weakening your security.

We strongly recommend setting up a recovery method at this stage — see "I forgot my vault PIN" below for why this matters.

Security & Privacy

How CipherPic protects your information.

🔒 Is my data stored on your servers?

No. In Offline Mode, your encrypted vaults remain only on your device. CipherPic is built as a local-first application, so your protected content — photos, notes, passwords, and documents — is not routinely uploaded, copied, or monitored on our servers.

The only exception is if you choose to use an optional, clearly labeled server-assisted sharing feature — see Secure Sharing for details.

🔑 I forgot my vault PIN.

Use your recovery method if you configured one, such as a recovery code or security questions. If you did not set up a recovery method, your encrypted data may not be recoverable — by us or anyone else.

This is a deliberate part of CipherPic's design: because we never receive or store your PIN or encryption keys, we have no way to unlock a vault on your behalf. We strongly recommend setting up a recovery method as soon as you create your vault.

🎭 What is Camouflage Mode?

Camouflage Mode disguises CipherPic's app icon and interface as another everyday app — such as a calculator or weather app — for additional privacy on a shared or borrowed device. The vault itself remains protected by your PIN or biometric unlock underneath the disguise.

🚨 What is a Duress PIN?

A Duress PIN is a special PIN you configure that opens a decoy vault instead of your real vault. If you're ever asked or pressured to unlock CipherPic, entering your Duress PIN shows a separate, non-sensitive vault while your real content stays hidden and protected.

📸 Does CipherPic protect against screenshots?

CipherPic includes screenshot and screen-recording protection while viewing protected content, where supported by your device's operating system. This is intended as a deterrent rather than an absolute guarantee — no app can fully prevent capture by every possible method, such as a second device photographing the screen.

Vaults & Organization

Working with multiple vaults and organizing your content.

🗂️ Can I create more than one vault?

Yes. CipherPic supports multiple independent vaults, each with its own protection. This is useful for separating personal and shared content, or for using Hidden Vault and Decoy Vault features alongside your primary vault.

🗑️ How does Secure Trash work?

When you delete an item from a vault, it moves to Secure Trash rather than being removed immediately. It stays there until you permanently delete it, empty Secure Trash, or an automatic cleanup period you've configured expires. This gives you a safety net against accidental deletion.

📝 What can I store besides photos?

In addition to photographs and images, CipherPic vaults can hold secure notes, passwords and account credentials, payment and membership cards, identity documents, and clipboard entries you choose to save.

Secure Sharing

Sending protected content to other people.

📤 How does Secure Sharing work?

Images are shared as encrypted .cps files, which only authorized CipherPic users can open. Depending on the sharing mode you choose, the file may be protected by a password you set locally, or — for server-assisted shares — by controls such as expiry and view limits enforced through our servers.

↩️ Can I revoke a share after sending it?

It depends on the sharing mode. Locally shared files are password-protected, but once a copy is in someone else's hands, it can't be retrieved or revoked. Server-assisted shares support expiry, view limits, and revocation — but only against apps that respect those controls; we can't guarantee that a modified or offline app won't retain content.

For this reason, only share content with people you trust.

💧 What is watermarking, and does it stop screenshots?

CipherPic can add a visible or embedded watermark to shared images to discourage unauthorized redistribution. Watermarking is a deterrent, not a guarantee — it can potentially be removed or degraded through image editing, resizing, or recompression.

Backups & Data

Keeping your information safe over time.

💾 How do I back up my vault?

Open Settings and select Backup to create an encrypted backup file of your vault contents. You choose where the backup is saved — such as local storage or a cloud provider of your choice. CipherPic does not automatically upload backups anywhere.

♻️ How do I restore a backup on a new device?

Install CipherPic on your new device, then choose Restore from Backup and select your saved backup file. You'll need the PIN or password associated with that backup to decrypt it.

🧹 What happens if I uninstall the app?

Uninstalling CipherPic removes the app and its locally stored data from your device, including your vaults, unless you've created a backup beforehand. If you want to keep access to your content, create and securely store a backup before uninstalling.

⚠️ What does Emergency Wipe do?

Emergency Wipe permanently erases your vault contents from the device. It's designed for situations where you need to immediately remove sensitive data. This action cannot be undone unless you have a separate backup stored elsewhere.

Troubleshooting

Common issues and how to resolve them.

🔄 The app won't unlock with biometrics.

Try the following:

  • Confirm biometric authentication is enabled for CipherPic in your device settings.
  • Re-register your fingerprint or face in your device's system settings if it was recently changed.
  • Use your PIN as a fallback, then re-enable biometric unlock from within CipherPic's security settings.
🐞 I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us at cipherpicapp@gmail.com with a description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, your device model, operating system version, and CipherPic version. Screenshots or recordings help but aren't required.

🛡️ I found a security vulnerability.

We welcome responsible disclosure. Please email cipherpicapp@gmail.com with details of the issue and steps to reproduce it, rather than disclosing it publicly, so we can investigate and address it.
