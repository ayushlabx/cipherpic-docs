Help Center

Your guide to using CipherPic securely.


Frequently Asked Questions


🔒 Is my data stored on your servers?

No. In Offline Mode, your encrypted vaults remain only on your device.


📤 How does Secure Sharing work?

Images are shared as encrypted .cps files. Only authorized CipherPic users can open them.


🔑 I forgot my vault PIN.

Use your recovery method if you configured one. Otherwise, encrypted data may not be recoverable.


📱 Can I use CipherPic without the Internet?

Yes. Almost all vault features work completely offline.


🎭 What is Camouflage Mode?

It disguises CipherPic as another app, such as a Calculator or Weather app, for additional privacy.


🚨 What is a Duress PIN?

A special PIN that opens a decoy vault instead of your real vault when you're under pressure.


📋 How do I import files?

Tap the + button in the app and follow the Import Security Wizard.
